ABOUT

THE GAME

Sea Bound is a pixel-art action game where you play as a deep-sea diver dropped from a research vessel into the most dangerous waters on Earth. Armed only with a harpoon gun and your wits, you must fend off predators, collect samples and escape before your oxygen runs out.

Built with a love for retro aesthetics and modern game-feel, Sea Bound blends chunky pixel art with fluid controls and a pulse-pounding original soundtrack.

THE TEAM

Sea Bound is an indie project developed by a small team of ocean enthusiasts, pixel artists and weekend coders who believe the sea should be as terrifying in a game as it is in real life.
