ABOUT

THE GAME

Sea Bound is a pixel-art action game where you play as a deep-sea diver dropped from a research vessel into some of the most dangerous waters on Earth. Armed only with a harpoon gun and your wits, you must retrieve ancient treasure and fight you way to escape before your oxygen runs out.
